## Participation
Attendance and regular participation in our weekly class meetings and online discussions are required. In-class participation includes contributing to discussions, asking and answering questions, and actively engaging in individual and group activities. Participation in online discussions includes both your original posts and your responses to other students’ posts.

Half of the 100 participation points will be calculated based on your attendance and participation in weekly class meetings. The other 50 points will be calculated based on your participation in online discussions.

Each student may have up to **two excused absences** during the semester for illness or health reasons, conference attendance, or other personal circumstances. Documentation (such as a doctor’s note) is not required. For an absence to be excused, however, you must notify me **before the class meeting begins**. If an emergency or other circumstance makes advance notice impossible, please contact me as soon as reasonably possible afterward.

After the two excused absences have been used, each additional absence will result in a **10-point deduction from the 100 participation points**. Missing required online discussions will likewise result in a 10-point deduction.

Because this is a discussion- and activity-based course, regular attendance is essential to meeting the course learning objectives. **More than four class absences during the semester may result in a failing grade for the course**, regardless of the participation-point total.
